## Why this is the end rather than a milestone

The plan's artefacts are real and every one is commanded. **They are also all
infrastructure, and none of them is the product.** 42 capability rows; 17 `real`. 25 are
`mock`, `deferred` or `missing`, and 25 of those have no command that could contradict
them — which is the honest reading of `capability:evidence`'s 17/17: 17 things were
measured, not that the product works.

The gaps that decide it, in order:

1. **`auth.sessions` is `mock`.** There is no real authentication, so no one can be given
   access. This is the blocker, not one of 42 rows.
2. **Four of five agent domains still answer `501 AGENT_NOT_CONFIGURED`.** The
   `automation.agents` row is `real` on one domain, and its own prose says so.
3. **The axe sweep was never run** — 20 route files × 2 themes × 4 states, no numbers.
4. **The site is not deployed**, which is why SITE-1 stays `debt` and the workflow step
   is a harness rather than evidence.
5. **ADR-006 refuses multi-tenancy, hosted SaaS, iOS and a second language.** That is a
   deliberate cap on the addressable market, recorded as a decision, and it is one of the
   reasons the wedge this product could serve is narrower than the category it sits in.

## The one thing not to read into this commit

`pnpm verify:local` green is not product readiness. The gates here are unusually rigorous
and they grew over the same months the capability count did; the ratio between
engineering rigour and shipped capability is the reason this file exists rather than a
continuation plan. A reader who finds a fully green chain should read it as **a
well-built substrate with 25 capabilities missing**, because that is what it is.
